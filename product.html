Preparation Guide

1

Measure Ingredients

Take 2 scoops (approx 20g) of Fruitingo Nendran Banana powder in a clean cooking pan. Add 1 cup (200ml) of milk or water.

2

Whisk Out Lumps

Whisk the mixture thoroughly before heating. Ensure there are absolutely no dry lumps remaining in the pan.

3

Cook on Low Flame

Place the pan on low-to-medium heat. Stir continuously for 5 to 8 minutes. The mixture will begin to glaze and thicken.

4

Sweeten & Serve

Turn off heat. Add organic jaggery powder, dates syrup, or a teaspoon of ghee for flavor. Serve warm.
