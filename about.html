The Premium Nendran Legacy

Nendran plantains (French Plantain variety) are exclusive fruits cultivated majorly in Kerala and parts of Western Tamil Nadu. Known for their unique starch properties, these bananas are rich in nutrition and highly prized in Ayurveda.

We source raw, unripe bananas at the precise pre-ripening stage. This ensures the starch is resistant starch, acting as a prebiotic that feeds gut-healthy probiotics.

Sun-Dried & Cleanly Crafted

Traditional methods matter. Rather than relying on industrial dehydrators that operate at extreme temperatures and strip vital minerals, our bananas are micro-sliced and sun-dried in clean, enclosed solar domes.

This retains the bright pale yellow color of the bananas and seals the delicate sweetness and vitamins. No artificial colors, preservatives, or sweeteners are added. It's just 100% pure banana.

Our Sourcing & Packing Journey

We trace every single banana back to organic orchards in Western Ghats foothills.

1

Organic Farming

Naturally grown raw Nendran plantains cultivated by farmers under strict organic norms without chemical pesticides.

2

Manual Hand-Picking

Only raw green bananas at optimal starch content are selected, washed thoroughly in running clean water, and peeled.

3

Traditional Sun Drying

Bananas are cut into micro-slices and laid on pristine clean racks. They dry naturally under the hot tropical sun to retain maximum enzymes and nutrients.

4

Fine Milling & Sealing

Dried banana chips are milled into a silky soft flour, double tested for impurities, and sealed immediately in food-safe bags.

Frequently Asked Questions

All you need to know about preparing, storing, and buying Fruitingo Nendran Banana powder.

Is it safe to give Nendran Banana Powder to a 6-month-old baby?

Yes, absolutely. Raw Nendran Banana powder is a traditional first solid food recommended for babies in South India. It is highly digestible, hypoallergenic, and does not cause cold or cough, unlike regular bananas.

Are there any added sugars or artificial flavors?

No. Fruitingo Nendran Banana Powder contains exactly one ingredient: 100% raw Nendran bananas. We add zero white sugar, salt, milk solids, starch thickeners, or artificial colors.

How should I store the powder and what is the shelf life?

Our powder has a shelf life of 6 months from packaging. Once opened, transfer it to a dry, airtight jar. Store in a cool dry cupboard. You can also refrigerate it to preserve freshness longer in humid regions.

Does it help in baby weight gain?

Yes. Raw banana powder contains healthy complex carbohydrates and potassium that help in steady, natural weight gain in growing infants and toddlers, without causing bloating.
