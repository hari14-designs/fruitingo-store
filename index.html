Our Sourcing & Packing Journey

We trace every single banana back to organic orchards in Western Ghats foothills.

1

Organic Farming

Naturally grown raw Nendran plantains cultivated by farmers under strict organic norms without chemical pesticides.

2

Manual Hand-Picking

Only raw green bananas at optimal starch content are selected, washed thoroughly in running clean water, and peeled.

3

Traditional Sun Drying

Bananas are cut into micro-slices and laid on pristine clean racks. They dry naturally under the hot tropical sun to retain maximum enzymes and nutrients.

4

Fine Milling & Sealing

Dried banana chips are milled into a silky soft flour, double tested for impurities, and sealed immediately in food-safe bags.
